A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear from
the start.

Does the customer need WBT?+

No. The customer needs USDT and signs a payment authorization. Our relayer submits the transaction and pays gas in WBT.

What happens if my server goes down?+

Confirmed payments stay recorded. We retry the merchant notification when your endpoint is available. Your integration must fulfill idempotently and acknowledge completion.

Who holds the payment?+

The checkout contract forwards the payment directly to the merchant in the same transaction. It does not retain the payment as escrow.

Are refunds guaranteed?+

No. Refunds require the merchant's authorization and enough USDT in their wallet. The contract tracks the amount refunded and prevents refunding more than was paid.

Can I use it with real funds today?+

This release is for Whitechain Sepolia testnet. It is not audited for production funds. Explore the open-source integration and the recorded testnet transactions.
